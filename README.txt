** A Lame Party
Join the most boring hallowen party ever, collect and eat some food, then talk to the bored people attending to the party.
This game was made by Michel Redmer da Fonseca as a job application with NG+ and BGNSS Studios. It's meant to prove I'm a capable Unity Programmer/Developer.

** How to Play
Use keyboard and mouse

WASD to walk
MOUSE to look around and use inventory
F to interact with items and npcs
TAB to open inventory
ESC/F1 to open pause menu
